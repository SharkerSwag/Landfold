---
name: check-transect
description: Verify a claim with the selected map lines, recharge months, or a regional elevation profile in Landfold.
---

1. Use the latest user turn's `mode` and treat earlier selections as historical context.
   In map mode, call `sample_current_transect` for `currentLine`. Use its counts and ordered runs.
   When `referenceLine` is present, call `sample_reference_transect`. For comparisons, name the current and reference lines in the answer and give each count with its own denominator.
   In recharge mode, call `compare_recharge_months` for `selectedMonths` and `lookup_evidence` with ID `recharge`. Use the returned values and cite [recharge]. Report B minus A in percentage points. Treat the table as model-estimated proportions at the study sites and dates. A claim about water volume needs total volume data. A claim about another year or individual plot needs evidence for that year or plot.
   In combined mode, carry out both checks in the same turn. Cite [map] for the selected lines and [recharge] for the monthly calculation. The image line is sampled in the interface and has no month assigned. Never place the map readings in the months from the water table or at the isotope sampling sites. These observations cannot establish why a village was built there. Say what spatial, historical or hydrological evidence would test the proposed explanation.
   In terrain mode, call `read_terrain_profile` and look up [terrain]. Report the selected point and profile using the tool's distance and elevation. The SRTM profile has no aligned land-use data. Do not assign its points to a village or field, or infer water flow from the profile.
2. Call `lookup_evidence` for the claim being checked. Use `map` for land use, `water` for recharge, `photo` for field photography, and `region` for regional settlement or water management. Fetch multiple entries when a claim crosses scopes. Reuse tool results within the turn. Call each calculation once and look up each source ID at most once.
3. Answer in Chinese in at most three short paragraphs. Present the observations for the selected evidence, then assess the claim and state what additional evidence it needs. Cite returned evidence IDs in square brackets. In combined and terrain modes, the service prepends the readings from tool output. Write one short paragraph on the claim and its limits. Avoid repeating the readings or describing the map and table again. Keep the required citations in the interpretation.
4. Treat unmapped colors as unreadable. Use the line's sample counts as approximate chart readings. Report zero as “这条线未采到该地类” and preserve uncertainty from unreadable points. To assess relative elevation, require spatially aligned elevation and land-use data. To infer local water flow, require observations of the channel or hydrological evidence.
5. For questions about where along the line a class occurs, copy its matching ranges from `orderedRuns`, measured from A toward B. Report only those matching ranges. Do not calculate or describe the complementary ranges. For other questions, give counts and omit point ranges. Never infer adjacency or the reason for settlement location from counts.
