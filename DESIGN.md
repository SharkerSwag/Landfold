# Landfold design direction

## Decision

On 2026-10-27, the immersive photographic direction **B** was selected as the project's starting point. The English name is **Landfold**, with **地景志** retained as the Chinese name.

The public page is a focused implementation of this direction, not the multi-variant prototype promoted unchanged.

## Principles

- Authentic photography establishes the visual experience.
- Typography, composition and restrained interactions support close observation.
- Photographic details and sourced regional explanations remain distinguishable.
- Recommendations, when added, should focus on landscapes and geographical understanding.
- Tourism purchasing, itinerary commerce and booking flows are outside the current direction.
- The eventual Agent should support the reading experience without replacing the photography or exposing model credentials in public assets.

## Preserved design reference

The initial A/B/C comparison is retained on the separate [design/photographic-prototypes branch](https://github.com/SharkerSwag/Landfold/tree/design/photographic-prototypes/prototype). It preserves:

- A: paper-like editorial feature;
- B: immersive photographic cover;
- C: gallery-style geographical reading.

The prototype is a historical design reference. It is not part of the public Pages source or an alternative production frontend.

## Initial scope

One geographical feature, two attributed photographs, four observation topics, complete photo viewing, a source reader and a session-only notebook. Subsequent topics and Agent functionality can be added once this visual foundation is established.
